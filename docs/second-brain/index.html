Overview

A bilingual (EN/PT) portfolio for Ruben Costa, a full-stack developer looking for a first role, told as a minimal anime-style series (v3): hero → episodes 01–03 → quest log (projects) → skill tree → side quests → episode 04 "Brand new day" night scene → contact. v1 (a film) and v2 (a comic book) were replaced at Ruben's request; v3 keeps one quiet paper look and shows his love for games, code, Spider-Man and anime/manga in small details, with parallax and two-way scroll animations. It follows portfolio-redesign-playbook.md, with cinematic native-scroll motion (framer-motion) and nods to Spider-Man, Subaru Natsuki (Re:Zero), Fight Club and The Social Network.
